Can I reuse the animations on other pages?

Yes. The animation functions are exported from one module and can be applied with selectors or direct elements.

Does it support reduced motion?

Yes. Motion-heavy effects automatically resolve to static visible states for users who prefer reduced motion.

Can I change the visual style?

The palette, spacing, section classes, and motion settings are centralized so the template is straightforward to brand.
